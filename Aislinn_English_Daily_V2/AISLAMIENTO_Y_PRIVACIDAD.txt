AISLAMIENTO Y PRIVACIDAD - V2.0

Aislinn V2.0 is intentionally isolated.
- Microphone STT files live only under app\runtime and app\models.
- No Windows Speech Recognition capabilities are installed.
- No drivers are installed or removed.
- No default audio device is changed.
- No registry audio setting is changed.
- No PATH/environment variable is changed.
- No administrator permission is requested by the app or local microphone preparation.
- Ollama is accessed only on localhost and is never stopped/removed.
- Unreal, MetaHuman and .uproject files are not used.
- The app only terminates its own temporary whisper-stream child process when a listening operation times out/cancels.
